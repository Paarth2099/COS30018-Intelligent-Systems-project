# Final evaluation photo assignments

These are collection instructions, not completed work. Thirty new photos per writer; 120 total.

Do not view predictions before the collection is checked and locked. Do not reuse old handwriting or photograph the same old writing again. Leave paper margins; keep the entire item visible and in focus. Use separate, non-cursive characters on one line without spaces or punctuation. Photograph each whole string; do not compose it from digit tiles. Preserve originals and do not hand-crop them to improve predictions.

Save under `data/final_evaluation/raw/<Name>/<collection>/`. Use the filename stems below with the actual `.jpg`, `.jpeg` or `.png` extension; do not rename a JPEG extension to PNG. Labels are in the manifest so filenames need not encode case.

The assigned letters alone cover A–T and a–t; strings add U–Z and u–z. Coverage does not mean enough examples for reliable per-class claims.
